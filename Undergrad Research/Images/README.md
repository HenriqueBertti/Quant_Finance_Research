# Quantitative Risk & Research Visualizations

This directory contains visual outputs, empirical distribution plots, and econometric model evaluations supporting this research project.

### Key Visualizations:
- **Tail Risk & Extremes**: Empirical vs. theoretical distributions, Q-Q plots, and tail estimates under Extreme Value Theory (EVT).
- **Volatility Dynamics**: Conditional volatility estimates from GARCH/EGARCH specifications.
- **Multivariate Dependence**: Scatter plots and joint dependency structures fitted with copula models.
- **Backtesting & Diagnostics**: Performance curves, backtesting violations, and loss distribution forecasts.

All figures are automatically exported in high resolution for documentation, working papers, and presentation materials.
